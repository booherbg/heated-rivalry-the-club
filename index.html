On an iPhone: a one-minute setup

  1. Full screen. In Safari, tap Share (the square with the arrow pointing up), scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. Open The Club from your home screen: no bars at all.
  2. Brightness all the way up. Swipe down from the top-right corner and drag the sun slider to the top.
  3. Auto-Brightness off, or the dark room dims you: Settings, Accessibility, Display & Text Size, then turn off Auto-Brightness (at the very bottom).
  4. Low Power Mode off. It dims the screen and lets it sleep sooner: Settings, Battery.
  5. Do Not Disturb on, so texts don't pop up over the light: swipe down from the top-right corner, tap Focus, then Do Not Disturb.
  6. If the screen still goes to sleep: Settings, Display & Brightness, Auto-Lock, Never (just for tonight).
